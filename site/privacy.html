Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Klue (kluedaily.com) is a free daily game. There are no accounts, no ads and no sign-in. This page says what the site keeps and why.

On your device

Your guesses for today, your stats (played, win rate, streak) and your chosen filter are saved in your browser's local storage so they survive a refresh. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Usage analytics

We use PostHog (servers in the United States) to learn how the game is played, so we can tune difficulty and add songs. It records:

  • page views, with the referring site and campaign tags such as utm_source;
  • game events: starting a game, each guess, finishing (won or not, number of guesses, mode, filter, difficulty, which clue columns were shown), sharing, changing filter or mode, opening the clue explanations, and opening the error report link;
  • a random ID stored in local storage, so return visits can be counted, plus browser type, device type and an approximate location (country/city) derived from your IP address.

We don't use cookies, record sessions or your screen, sell data, or link the random ID to your name or email. We don't build advertising profiles.

To opt out, block posthog.com with a content blocker, or clear the site's local storage to reset the random ID.

Fonts

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it sends them.

Reporting a data error

The "Report a data error" link opens a Google Form. Only what you type into it is sent, and Google's privacy policy applies to the form.

Song data

The game shows facts about songs (release date, label, length, genre, view tier). It contains no audio, lyrics or album art. Song titles and artist names belong to their owners.
